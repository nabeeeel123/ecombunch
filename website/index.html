FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Do I need to show my face or record anything?

No. Most channels we run are faceless, using voiceover, stock and licensed footage, animation and motion graphics. If you want to be on camera, we can build around your recordings too.

Who owns the channel?

You do. The channel is created under your account and you keep full ownership of the channel, files and all revenue.

Is this allowed by YouTube and TikTok?

Yes, as long as the content is original and adds value, and that's the only kind we make. Channels that reupload other people's videos or mass-produce low-effort content are the ones that get demonetised. We follow YouTube's monetisation and inauthentic-content policies and TikTok's community guidelines.

How fast will I see results?

Most channels need 3–6 months of consistent uploads to find traction. Anyone who guarantees views or income is not being honest with you, so we don't. We do guarantee consistent, quality output and transparent reporting.

Can I approve videos before they go live?

Yes. You can approve topics, scripts and final edits, or let us run it and just review the weekly report. It's up to you.

Do you work with existing channels?

Yes. We audit your current channel, find what's holding it back and take over the parts of production you want off your plate.
